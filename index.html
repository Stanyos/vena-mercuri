Philosophy & Approach

Venamercuri reads charts the way a translator reads an old language — not to predict, but to name what’s already moving underneath. The work spans the practical and the unprovable: a class that teaches you to read your own placements, a session that puts your body somewhere quieter than thought, a portrait built from the frequency of your chart rather than your face.

None of it asks you to believe anything in particular. It only asks you to look closely at what’s already written.

The approach stays the same regardless of the medium: look at what’s actually there, say it plainly, and leave room for the client to disagree. Chart work should clarify a life, not replace the living of it.
