1. What This Covers

This policy explains what information is collected through this website, how it's used, and how it's protected. It applies to the site itself and to the intake form used to request Services.

2. Information Collected

The intake form on this site asks for information you choose to provide, which may include:

  • Your name and email address
  • Date, time, and place of birth (for chart-based Services only)
  • Any details you share about what you're hoping to explore

Submitting the intake form transmits your inquiry securely over an encrypted connection directly to my private inbox via contact@venamercuri.nfinitemindai.com. No form data is sold or retained in browser local storage after delivery.

3. How Information Is Used

Any details sent by email are used solely to respond to your inquiry and, if a Service is subsequently arranged, to prepare your chart or reading and coordinate scheduling and payment directly with you. Birth details are used only for the specific reading, class, or portrait they were provided for.

4. Data Retention

Email correspondence and associated chart data are kept only as long as reasonably needed to provide the Service and maintain client records, and are deleted on request.

5. Sharing of Information

Your information is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes. It may be disclosed only where required by law.

6. Cookies and Analytics

This site does not currently use cookies or third-party analytics or tracking scripts. If that changes, this policy will be updated to reflect it.

7. Your Rights

You may request a copy of, correction to, or deletion of any personal information held about you by emailing contact@venamercuri.nfinitemindai.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated periodically. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@venamercuri.nfinitemindai.com.
